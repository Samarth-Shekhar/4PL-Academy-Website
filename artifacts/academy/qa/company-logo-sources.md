## Verification

- All eight local files returned HTTP 200 and were validated as SVG or PNG.
- The homepage section was visually inspected at desktop and mobile widths.
- The strip's transform changes while animating; reduced-motion preference
  stops the animation and removes the duplicate logos.
- The website build and JavaScript checks passed. Existing certification
  records remain conditional on verification; the logo strip remains visible
  independently of their presence.
