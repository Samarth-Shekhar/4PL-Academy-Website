marketing and finance to operations, customer experience, human resources and

Students build strong foundations in management, marketing, finance,
economics and entrepreneurship while learning how Artificial Intelligence,
analytics, automation and digital technologies are changing the way
organisations operate and compete.

The objective is not to turn business students into software engineers. It is to
develop business professionals who understand how to work with Al, make
better decisions with technology and lead digital transformation.
