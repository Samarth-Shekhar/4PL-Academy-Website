The owner wants the whole website to emphasize AI courses and AI imagery, using the existing blue-and-gold palette and supplied academy data.

**Why:** The owner requested a stronger visual redesign inspired by both EduNova and Studify, rather than only recoloring the previous layout.

**How to apply:** Use bold educational typography, dimensional imagery and smooth, lightweight entrance/scroll transitions. Keep logistics and the other supplied offerings accessible; visual emphasis does not authorize invented curricula, outcomes or testimonial ratings.
