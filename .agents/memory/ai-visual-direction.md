name: AI visual direction
description: Owner's reference designs and emphasis for the academy's public presentation.
---

The owner wants the whole website to emphasize AI courses and AI imagery, using the existing blue-and-gold palette and supplied academy data.

**Why:** The owner requested a stronger visual redesign inspired by both EduNova and Studify, rather than only recoloring the previous layout.

**How to apply:** Use bold educational typography, dimensional imagery and smooth, lightweight entrance/scroll transitions. Keep logistics and the other supplied offerings accessible; visual emphasis does not authorize invented curricula, outcomes or testimonial ratings.

Use detailed AI subject imagery rather than generic AI bubbles or icon-only decorations. Keep hero text and imagery in separate layout areas with no overlap.

**Why:** The owner rejected generic bubble/icon treatment and headline overlap, then clarified that unboxed, moving brain and AI-chip artwork is preferred over the framed robot portrait.

**How to apply:** Use dimensional brain/chip cutouts with restrained motion and no background box for the hero, plain eyebrow text rather than a pill, and relevant robotics imagery elsewhere. Preserve student and university identity imagery.

- https://dribbble.com/shots/27029330-Education-Website-UI-Studify-Learning-Platform-for-Real-Growth

Keep the header and footer minimal and EduNova-inspired, preserving the full supplied footer data. Keep the hero eyebrow close to the headline and make logo/back-to-top scrolling smooth.

**Why:** The owner specifically requested a simpler header/footer and less hero spacing, alongside smooth downward entrances and upward navigation rather than abrupt jumps.

**How to apply:** Retain actual navigation, office/contact/social/legal details; use restrained transitions with reduced-motion support rather than blocking native scrolling.

Favor real photographs of humans learning robotics or working with technology in About, student life and editorial defaults, instead of warehouse/logistics stock imagery. Use the supplied 4PL Consultancy logo in “Why 4PL Consultancy,” not the Academy logo.

**Why:** The owner asked for human-and-AI EdTech imagery and supplied the parent consultancy's separate logo for that specific section.

**How to apply:** Preserve institutional campus identity, original blog artwork and the actual logistics offerings. Record stock-photo sources internally without claiming the people or facilities belong to the academy.
