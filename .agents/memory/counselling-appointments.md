---
name: Counselling appointment requests
description: Why counselling calendar selections are preferences, with an explicit time zone.
---

The counselling calendar captures a preferred appointment date/time in India Standard Time. It does not reserve a slot or imply that an advisor is available.

**Why:** The owner asked for date/time selections to be retained in the counselling leads and emailed in full, but did not supply advisor availability or authorize an appointment-reservation system.

**How to apply:** Keep the selected time zone with the date and time through submission, storage, staff editing and email. Clearly label the public calendar's zone; do not silently reinterpret times using the visitor's browser locale or phone country.
