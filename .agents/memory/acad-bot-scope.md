---
name: Acad Bot scope
description: Academy-focused assistance and the owner's required human-contact fallback.
---

Acad Bot helps visitors with 4PL Academy requirements, programs, courses,
universities and admissions. Questions outside that scope should receive a
polite request to contact the team and a working contact button, not unrelated
answers.

**Why:** The owner explicitly requested academy-focused assistance and a
contact-team response for unrelated questions.

**How to apply:** Preserve that boundary when changing retrieval, adding an
AI provider or expanding the bot. Unsupported information should also direct
visitors to the team rather than inventing details.

The bot's contact-team button must navigate directly to Contact Us and close
the assistant, rather than showing another panel with addresses or phone numbers.

**Why:** The owner explicitly requested direct page navigation instead of an
extra location-information popup.

**How to apply:** Keep human-contact fallback navigation distinct from the
optional admissions callback form.
