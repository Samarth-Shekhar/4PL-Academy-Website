contact information on the legacy academy website.

**Why:** The owner supplied a current academy email and Indian phone number
that differ from the legacy contact page. Reimporting that page must not undo
their correction.

page, footer and bot. Do not replace them with historical contact values during
content imports or repeat seeding.

embeds or using approximate city-centre coordinates as a precise office pin.

**Why:** The old site contains multiple historic map embeds, while stored
coordinates are explicitly approximate.

**How to apply:** Verify that the map resolves to the current building and
preserve floor/unit details in the displayed postal address.

The contact page should follow the owner's supplied split contact/form
reference, using 4PL Academy navy and gold rather than the reference's black
palette.

**Why:** The owner requested the same type of contact page with the current
academy blue and gold contrast.

**How to apply:** Preserve the reference-based layout and readable brand
contrast when refining the page, while retaining authoritative office details
and the actual street-address map.
