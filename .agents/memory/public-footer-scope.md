---
name: Public footer scope
description: Owner-requested public footer content exclusions.
---

Keep the public footer compact, without the admissions/employment-outcomes
disclaimer paragraph or a staff sign-in link.

**Why:** The owner specifically requested removing both from the public footer.

**How to apply:** Preserve the public contact, privacy and terms links. Removing
staff navigation must not remove or weaken staff authentication.

Use the owner's supplied blue study/library artwork for the footer background.

**Why:** The owner supplied this specific image and explicitly asked to use it.

**How to apply:** Preserve the supplied artwork when refining footer layout
or content; do not substitute a different stock or generated background.

Use bold text in the public header navigation and footer.

**Why:** The owner explicitly requested making both header and footer text bold.

**How to apply:** Keep desktop links, dropdowns and mobile navigation bold,
as well as footer links and contact details. Preserve current gold active
states, compact structure and footer artwork.
