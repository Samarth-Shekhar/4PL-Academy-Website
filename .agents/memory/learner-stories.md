When approved student reviews are unavailable, use 4PL Academy's own thoughts
about students and learning, not fictional first-person learner stories.

**Why:** After seeing the labelled samples, the owner clarified: "just write
some thoughts about our students- not example".

**How to apply:** Keep academy reflections in the academy's voice, without
sample badges, invented student identities or claims of achieved outcomes.
Use approved student quotes for genuine testimonials, keeping them distinct
from the academy's own views.

Show star ratings only when the student supplied a rating; never add a default score or attach stars to academy reflections.

**Why:** The owner requested rated testimonials in the AI-focused redesign, but no approved ratings were supplied with the existing content.
