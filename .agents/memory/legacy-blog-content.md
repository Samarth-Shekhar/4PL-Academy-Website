description: Original blog imagery/proportions and reliable title authority for legacy 4PL content.

Use the original legacy artwork for migrated blog posts and preserve each image's
intrinsic proportions across the listing, article pages and reusable blog cards.
Do not replace it with generated imagery or crop it into a different ratio.

**Why:** The user requested all old 4PL Academy blog data and the exact images
and ratios.

Use the legacy blog listing as the title authority when a detail page has a
copy-and-paste headline error. Preserve the original detail heading in source
metadata rather than silently assigning the wrong topic to a post.

**Why:** The festive-season logistics detail page carried an unrelated
India–Russia headline despite having the correct festive-season article body.

**How to apply:** Compare listing and detail metadata during future imports,
retain original dates and authors, and separate article text from shared chat
widgets and form messages.

The legacy public host has exhibited an incomplete TLS certificate chain.

**Why:** Verified direct HTTPS retrieval failed while the public content was
otherwise accessible.

**How to apply:** Try normal certificate verification first. Keep any necessary
retrieval exception restricted to fixed, read-only public source assets; never
relax application or backend TLS. Validate image content before importing.
