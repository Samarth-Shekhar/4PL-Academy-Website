The About Us page must show every section name fully, with all section content expanded. Its layout should resemble the original website's open image-and-text sections, rather than putting everything in cards.

**Why:** The user repeated “make the full sections visible” after a horizontal section-link strip cut off the later names, and asked us to follow the original About Us page with images.

**How to apply:** Keep every section link readable on desktop and phones without requiring horizontal scrolling to discover hidden names. Preserve the full source content and pair suitable images with the text.
