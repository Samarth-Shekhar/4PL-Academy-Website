Keep only the university directory view; do not reintroduce a separate verified-partnerships view or partnership filter.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested “I only want university directory.”

**How to apply:** Keep institution discovery separate from affiliation claims. An original institution logo identifies the institution; it does not establish a 4PL partnership or change academic-record verification.

Listing cards should share a consistent image ratio and show only basic information supplied by the owner or source records.

**Why:** The user requested matching templates and basic supplied information instead of lengthy cards and repetitive missing-information placeholders.

**How to apply:** Omit unavailable facts from cards, retain complete supplied information on detail pages, and align card actions.

Homepage program previews should contain only concrete source-supported
university/program pairs, not unlinked titles or inferred “Undergraduate
programs at…” entries. Keep them compact: matching image ratio, university
name and program title, followed by a “Check all programs” link.

**Why:** The owner rejected the oversized cards with random imagery and
requested small, consistent cards showing only supplied university/program
information and real university images from the web.

**How to apply:** Use matching campus imagery, not generic industry pictures.
Retain full supplied information in the full catalog and detail pages; a
homepage preview must not invent university associations or course titles.

Combine homepage university discovery into the All / Undergraduate / Postgraduate showcase, rather than a separate Discover Universities section. Cards in this showcase must share fixed overall and image ratios.

**Why:** The owner asked to remove the pictured university section, combine its cards with the degree section or an All view, and keep every card's size and ratio consistent.

**How to apply:** University-only cards can appear in All when no concrete source-supported program pair is available. Keep degree-specific views limited to actual supplied programs; do not infer degree offerings for bare institution cards.
