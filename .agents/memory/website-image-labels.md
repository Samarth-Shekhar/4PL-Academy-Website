---
name: Website imagery rules
description: Subject-appropriate imagery, distinct navy photographic page headers, and no visible illustrative captions.
---

Do not show “Representative generic imagery”, “Illustrative imagery · Details”,
“Illustrative photography…” or equivalent illustrative/generic-image captions beneath images or in the hero
across this website.

**Why:** The user asked to remove these labels all over the website, then pointed
out additional hero labels that had been missed.

**How to apply:** Check shared image-caption components and hero controls as well
as individual pages and CMS-sourced descriptions. Keep factual source/provenance metadata and do not describe
generic artwork as a verified campus, person or event.

For the homepage's hero and program-promotion imagery, use program subjects
and objects rather than learner portraits. Retain animation, large clean text
and the Student Grad screenshot design direction, adapted to 4PL Academy.

**Why:** After the first program carousel used learners, the user clarified that
the images should be of programs, not humans, and supplied the intended style.

**How to apply:** Show subjects such as AI hardware, logistics infrastructure,
digital marketing equipment and trade materials in program-promotion imagery.
This does not prohibit student photography in student-life content.

Use realistic human student photography in "Learning Happens Together".
Give learning and admission carousel items distinct images rather than
recycling the program hero's artwork.

**Why:** The owner explicitly requested human student images in this section
and different, previously unused images for the journey cards.

**How to apply:** Keep the imagery distinct across these sections. Retain
honest source metadata for stock photos and do not label them as actual 4PL
campus events or identified academy learners.

Keep hero navigation minimal: no hero search field or previous/next arrow
buttons; retain small clickable slide indicators for manual selection.

**Why:** The owner explicitly requested removing the search bar and arrows,
leaving only small pointers to choose slides.

**How to apply:** Preserve indicator selection and automatic hero transitions
when refining the hero; do not reintroduce those removed controls.

Keep the homepage's "Your Next Chapter Starts Here" closing call-to-action
on a plain white background, with navy-blue text and gold accents.

**Why:** The owner explicitly requested replacing that section's dark image
background with white and blue/gold styling.

**How to apply:** Keep this exception distinct from the image-based hero and
the owner's supplied footer artwork; preserve its existing CTA destinations.

Do not show the “Life at 4PL” gallery section on the homepage. Keep the
“Learning Happens Together” images large rather than restricting them to
a narrow column beside the heading.

**Why:** The owner asked to remove the gallery block and enlarge the learning
images shown in their screenshots.

**How to apply:** Preserve gallery records and the student-life route, but
omit that homepage block. Keep the learning images prominent and responsive.

Student Life and Partner With Us must use distinct imagery, not the same shared photo set.

**Why:** The user asked for different Student Life images and unique Partner With Us images.

**How to apply:** Select appropriate student and professional photographs separately, maintaining honest source metadata and avoiding claims that stock scenes depict actual academy events.

Pages reached through the main header should share the photographic navy-blue overlay style of Partner With Us, with a different relevant image for each destination.

**Why:** The user requested “same kind bg but different images for every header section when clicked.”

**How to apply:** Keep destination images distinct across navigation pages and course-dropdown categories. Preserve the homepage's existing course-focused rotating hero; aliases for the same page should retain the same image.
