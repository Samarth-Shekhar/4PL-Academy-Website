name: Public browsing scope
description: Owner's requirements for visible listing filters and removal of the public Apply section.
---

Place listing refinement filters at the top, above the results, rather than in a left sidebar.

**Why:** The user asked to make the pictured Refine section “on top which is easier to see not on left side.”

**How to apply:** Keep filtering controls visible above program, course and university listings across screen sizes.

Remove the Apply section and Apply links throughout the public website.

**Why:** The user said “remove apply section from everywhere.”

**How to apply:** Do not reintroduce Apply into public navigation, promotional buttons or journey sections. Existing stored enquiry records are not part of this public presentation change.

Do not embed an enquiry form on Student Life.

**Why:** The user subsequently asked to remove the form from Student Life.

**How to apply:** Keep the page focused on its informational content and images; enquiries can continue through the existing contact and counselling pages.
