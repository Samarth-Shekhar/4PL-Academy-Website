---
name: Logo verification
description: Avoid incorrect scraped brands and visually incomplete logo assets.
---

Verify each logo's identity and visible artwork, not just its URL, image dimensions or successful loading.

**Why:** Institution websites also contain accreditation, press and employer logos. Filename-based scraping selected these unrelated brands. Some SVGs loaded successfully while their wordmarks remained invisible.

**How to apply:** Prefer the institution's actual header branding; visually compare the result with its name. Check light wordmarks on a contrasting background. Use a complete, authentic raster reproduction when an SVG is not self-contained or does not render fully. Preserve reviewed sources rather than overwriting them with new scraped guesses.

Campus-image search results also need visual identity and subject checks.

**Why:** An official “Green Campus” result was an event group portrait rather
than a building photograph; another correctly named result was a very small
panorama. A recognizable building image was available from the academy's
existing university source.
Official marketing assets can also be raster masks or student photographs
rather than views of the institution. Successful decoding is not enough.

**How to apply:** Check the actual image before displaying it as a campus.
Prefer visible institution signage or official aerial/building photography
and retain its source. Do not use event portraits or another institution's
image merely because a search result contains the requested university name.
